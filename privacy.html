Privacy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you visit anthonymcneill.com or email me. The site is run by Anthony McNeill, a sole proprietor in Maryland ("I", "me").

The short version

  • This site has no forms, accounts, advertising trackers or chat tools, and it does not use cookies.
  • The only personal information I get from you is what you choose to email me.
  • I use it to reply and to talk about your business. I do not sell it.
  • Please do not email passwords, or health, card, bank or Social Security details.

1. What I collect

If you email me: your name, email address and business name, plus anything else you put in your message or email signature.

If we have a video call: the video service handles your name, email address and the call itself. I record a call only if everyone on it agrees first.

If someone introduces you to me, or I find your business's public contact details: your name, business name, contact details and when we were in touch.

When you visit the site: like any website, the site's host automatically handles technical details needed to deliver pages and keep the site secure, such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time. I see only overall visit counts, not who visited.

This policy does not cover work I do for clients. That is covered by our written agreement.

2. How I use it

I use your information to:

  • reply to you and arrange a video call if we both want one;
  • contact you about my services after an introduction, or at your business's public address;
  • prepare a quote or written scope you ask for;
  • keep simple records of my enquiries and business;
  • keep the site working and secure; and
  • follow the law and protect my legal rights.

I will not add you to a mailing list because you emailed me.

3. Who sees it

I do not sell your personal information, and I do not share it for anyone's advertising. It reaches:

  • Service providers that run the site, my email and video calls: GitHub for the site, Zoho for my business email, and Google Meet for video calls. They handle it to provide their services to me.
  • Authorities or others, if needed: when the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety or my legal rights.
  • A company I own: if I move this business into a company I own, such as a limited liability company, your information moves with it and this policy continues to apply until it is changed.

4. AI tools

I may use AI writing tools to help me organize enquiries and draft replies, and I review what goes out. I do not put your information into any tool that uses it to train its models.

5. Cookies and tracking

This site does not use cookies, advertising pixels or analytics that identify you. No other company collects information about your activity on this site over time or across other websites. Because I do not track you, the site works the same way whether or not your browser sends a "Do Not Track" or similar signal.

6. How long I keep it

  • Enquiries that do not become a job: up to 24 months after our last contact, unless you ask me to delete them sooner.
  • Businesses I contacted that did not reply: up to 6 months after my last message.
  • If you ask me not to contact you: I keep your address and the date for as long as I contact businesses directly, so I can keep honoring your request.
  • Clients: as stated in our written agreement, and as long as tax and legal record-keeping requires.
  • Technical data held by the host: under the host's own retention rules.

7. Your choices

You can email me at tony@anthonymcneill.com to ask what information I have about you, to correct it, or to delete it. I will answer within 45 days. I may need to confirm that the request is really from you, and I may keep what the law requires me to keep, such as tax records, and a do-not-contact record. I will not treat you differently for asking.

8. Security

I use reasonable steps to protect what you send, but email is not a secure way to send sensitive information, and no system is perfectly safe. If something sensitive reaches me by mistake, I will tell you and delete it.

9. Children

This site is meant for adults running businesses. It is not directed to children, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you think a child has emailed me, tell me and I will delete the message.

10. Other websites

If this site links to another website, that website has its own privacy policy.

11. Changes

If I change this policy, I will post the new version here with a new effective date. If a change is significant, I will also put a clear notice on the site before it takes effect.

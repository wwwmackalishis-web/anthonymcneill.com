Common questions

Do callers know it's an AI?

Yes. Every agent I deliver says it's an AI assistant in the greeting and says so if asked. I don't build agents that pretend to be human. If you edit the agent later, please keep that greeting.

Is the call recorded?

Most voice platforms record and transcribe calls, so the agent gives a recording notice and asks permission at the very start of the call. If the caller says no, it offers a callback instead. Recording laws vary by state, and some, including Maryland, generally require every party's consent, so please confirm your own requirements. This isn't legal advice.

Is it an emergency line?

No. When a caller mentions an emergency from your list, it tells them to call 911 or transfers them to your on-call number. Please keep a way for callers to reach a person.

What does it cost?

Fixed one-time prices from $265 to $1,275, plus applicable taxes, depending on your type of business and package. See the packages above. You also pay your own voice platform and phone costs directly. After a video call, your written scope sets the final price for your job.

When would it go live?

Not right away. Your written scope sets out what gets built, what has to happen first, including testing and your approval, and the timing. Nothing goes live on your phone line until those steps are done.

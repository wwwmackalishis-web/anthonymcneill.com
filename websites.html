You own it

You own your hosting and your domain. You buy them and pay those providers directly. I don't offer hosting.

I hold no login to your domain, DNS, Google or listings accounts, and no owner or billing role on your hosting. If publishing needs access, you invite me to that one new site project, named in your written scope, and you remove the invite at handover.

How the coached session works

We meet by video call, in one or two sittings. You set up or claim your Google Business Profile and up to 3 listings in your own accounts, typing everything yourself while I coach. Afterward you get a one-page note of what was set up.

Google decides whether a business qualifies for a Business Profile. Verification is your step, on Google's timetable. If it isn't finished in the session, you finish it from written steps. Listings we don't reach in the 2 hours are extra, quoted in writing first.

Common questions

You get an edit guide with your site. There's no monthly plan. If you want me to make a change later, I'll quote a fixed amount in writing, and work starts only after you say yes in writing. Bugs that stop what I delivered from working as described in your scope are fixed free for 30 days after delivery.

How do I get started?

Email me about your business. After a video call, you get a written scope. One named person approves the work for your business, and the dates go in the scope.

When do I use the consulting time?

It covers the site, the profile and the listings. You can use it in 30-minute steps, up to 30 days after handover. Time not used by then expires.
